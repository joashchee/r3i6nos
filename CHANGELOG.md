## Unreleased (0.1.1)

- **Commander's viewer opens Aseprite sprites** (`.ase`, `.aseprite`; 2026-11-13, ROADMAP section 1; `apps/commander-native/ase.h`, `make_ase.py`, `tests/test_ase.sh`): the Picture tab composites the visible layers of a frame (raw, zlib and linked cels; layer and cel opacity and offsets; the background flag; RGBA, grayscale and indexed through the new or old palette chunk; the 15 separable blend modes), **Left/Right step the frames** on a sprite with more than one (X/Y change the tab), and Info gives the size, depth, every frame's time, the layers, the tags, the palette and the transparent index. Tilemap layers, hue, saturation, colour and luminosity blends and a group's opacity or blend mode are not drawn, and the foot and Info's **Not drawn** line say so. Written from the public format description, no library; the fixtures are made in code (no Aseprite-made file is in the repo) and every probed pixel of every frame is checked against a plain composite written in the script; cut-short and damaged files run under ASan/UBSan (`scripts/check.sh` step). The corpus has `images/sprite.aseprite` (253 files). **Untested on the unit:** the speed of a big sprite sheet and how the frame stepping feels on the pad.

- **Commander plays MIDI, modules and music as text through nttplay** (2026-11-13, maintainer's request; `apps/commander-native/player.h` `PL_NTT`, `commander.c` `play_mode`, `apps/README.md`, ROADMAP section 27): the Play tab opens MIDI, RMID, MOD, S3M, XM, IT, MTM, STM, 669, MED, OKT, UMF, other tracker formats and ABC, MusicXML, MML and the other music-as-text kinds. It runs `nttplay probe FILE` first (the length for the progress bar, or nttplay's own reason, shown on Info when the file cannot be read), then `nttplay decode FILE -ar 44100`, read from a pipe like ffmpeg's sound; the finder is shared with ffmpeg's (`R3I6NOS_NTTPLAY` names another binary). With no nttplay, Info says so and the file opens as text or bytes as before. The selftest has a stand-in `nttplay` (length from probe, the sound, a refused file, no nttplay, nothing left running after closing); built and self-tested in Debian 12 arm64 (`-Werror`). **Not run on a unit:** whether MIDI keeps up through the pipe while Commander draws (the unit renders MIDI 3.4 times real time), the time `probe` takes, and the sound in step.
