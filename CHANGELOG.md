- **Commander opens files inside archives, and unpacks xz and zstd** (2026-11-10, maintainer's request; `apps/README.md`, "Files inside archives"; `ROADMAP.md` section 1). On the viewer's Contents tab of a ZIP (and `.love`, jar/apk, msix), a TAR (plain, `.tar.gz`, `.tar.xz`, `.tar.zst`), an ar archive or a `.deb`, one member is picked (Up/Down, L1/R1 page): **A** takes it out into a temporary file and opens it in the viewer (a picture, text, or another archive, up to 4 deep; **B** returns to the same member and the file is removed), **Start** extracts it to the other panel's folder under its own name and never over a file that is there. New: `tests/test_unpack.sh` (616 cases against the real `xz` and `zstd` under the sanitizers, in `scripts/check.sh`; CI installs both), eight new self-test fixtures (`make_selftest_data.py`, which now also needs `zstd`; the other fixtures were regenerated with Pillow 11) and 40 new self-test checks. - **Sound works again: alsa-lib refused the Boost volume** (2026-11-10, reported by the maintainer: every app said "cannot open: ALSA: Couldn't open audio device"; `docs/SOUND.md`, `docs/hardware-findings.md`). `/etc/asound.conf`'s Boost softvol had `min_dB 0.0`, and alsa-lib 1.2.8 refuses a softvol whose `min_dB` is not negative, so no program could open the default device (reproduced in Debian 12 under Docker with the image's alsa-lib). Boost now runs from -3 to +18 dB in 8 steps: step 0 is a softvol's silence and is never used, step 1 is exactly 0 dB (passed through untouched), the first boot's +6 dB is step 3. `r3i6nos-sound` and Settings > Sound mixer stop Boost at step 1, a saved Boost of 0 is put back to 1 at boot, and the mixer shows Volume's step 0 as "off". New `tests/test_asound_conf.py` checks every softvol against alsa-lib's rules and, where `aplay` is installed, opens the chain in alsa-lib (it fails on the config that shipped); CI now installs `alsa-utils` for it. Untested on the unit.
